Case Study 3

Paid social, email and SEO that lifted program conversions by 40%

Integrated digital marketing for TechReady Women Academy, an online learning platform for women entrepreneurs building startups.

WomenEducationDigital

Services: Paid social · Email automation · SEO · Content · Analytics and reporting

The Challenge

Turning interest into enrolments

TechReady Women Academy wanted more of the women who discovered its programs to go on and enrol, across regional NSW and metropolitan Sydney, and to grow its reach through search, email and new markets.

What I Did

One joined-up approach across channels

  • Ran geo-targeted campaigns across regional NSW and metropolitan Sydney, split-testing Facebook ads to improve performance
  • Set up marketing automation and personalised email campaigns in Mailchimp
  • Optimised the website for search, with keyword research and reporting in Google Analytics
  • Supported the move into international markets such as India with competitor analysis and Facebook campaigns
  • Created member-led content from participant testimonials and success stories
  • Managed the marketing budget and stakeholder coordination
The Result

More enrolments, more opens, more visitors

  • Program conversions increased by 40% through marketing automation and geo-targeted campaigns
  • Email open rates improved by 20% through personalised content
  • Organic traffic grew by 30% in five months through website optimisation and SEO

Ongoing PartnershipCase Study 1

Full digital ecosystem for a 50-year community centre

An ongoing digital partnership with Healesville Living & Learning Centre: building their digital presence, engaging their community and measuring their impact.

Community SectorNeighbourhood HouseVictoria

Services: Website management · SEO · Social media · Email marketing · Google Analytics · Facebook advertising · Digital strategy

The Challenge

A beloved community hub with an outdated digital presence

Healesville Living & Learning Centre has served the local community for over 50 years, running programs, events and services for people of all ages. But their digital presence wasn't keeping up.

  • Outdated website with broken links and poor mobile experience
  • Inconsistent brand across social media and printed materials
  • No analytics to understand what was working
  • No email marketing strategy to keep the community informed
  • Limited social media presence and no advertising strategy
What I Do

An ongoing digital partnership across every channel

I work closely with the HLLC team as their dedicated digital partner, managing their online presence end to end so they can focus on what they do best: serving their community.

Website & SEO

Migrating to a new WordPress site and keeping it current, accessible and mobile-friendly, with on-page SEO to improve search visibility.

Social Media

A monthly social media strategy, Facebook posts and advertising campaigns, with on-brand graphics made in Canva.

Email Newsletters

Monthly community e-newsletters sent through Mailchimp, with open and click tracking and a growing subscriber list.

Analytics & Reporting

Google Analytics set up and managed, with monthly reports on website traffic, social reach and email results.

Digital Strategy

A quarterly digital marketing strategy aligned to program and event goals, plus advice on tools and platforms.

CRM & Communications

Social Planet CRM integrated with the website, with contact lists, segmentation and reporting kept in order.

The Result

A thriving digital presence that serves the community

  • A new, up-to-date website that reflects the centre's programs and services
  • A consistent, engaging social media presence
  • Regular newsletters keeping subscribers informed and connected
  • Clear analytics and reporting giving the team visibility of what's working
  • A cohesive digital strategy aligned to HLLC's community goals
  • Improved search visibility through ongoing SEO work

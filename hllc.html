Ongoing Partnership Case Study 1

Full digital ecosystem for a 50-year community centre

An ongoing digital partnership with Healesville Living & Learning Centre — building their digital presence, engaging their community, and measuring their impact.

Community Sector Neighbourhood House Victoria

Services: Website management · SEO · Social media · Email marketing · Google Analytics · Facebook advertising · Digital strategy

The Challenge

A beloved community hub with an outdated digital presence

Healesville Living & Learning Centre has served the local community for over 50 years — running programs, events, and services for people of all ages. But their digital presence wasn't keeping up.

  • Outdated website with broken links and poor mobile experience
  • Inconsistent brand across social media and printed materials
  • No analytics to understand what was working
  • No email marketing strategy to keep the community informed
  • Limited social media presence and no advertising strategy
What I Do

An ongoing digital partnership across every channel

I work closely with the HLLC team as their dedicated digital partner — managing their online presence end-to-end so they can focus on what they do best: serving their community.

🌐

Website Management & SEO

  • Maintaining and updating their WordPress website
  • Ensuring pages are current, accessible and mobile-friendly
  • On-page SEO to improve search visibility
  • Monitoring site performance and fixing issues
📱

Social Media Management

  • Developing monthly social media strategy
  • Drafting and publishing Facebook posts
  • Creating on-brand graphics using Canva
  • Facebook advertising campaigns
📧

Email Marketing & Newsletters

  • Drafting monthly community newsletters
  • Publishing and distributing via Mailchimp
  • Analysing open rates, clicks and engagement
  • Growing and managing the subscriber list
📊

Analytics & Reporting

  • Setting up and managing Google Analytics
  • Monthly performance reports for the team
  • Tracking website traffic, social reach and email metrics
  • Using data to inform content and strategy decisions
🎯

Digital Strategy

  • Developing quarterly digital marketing strategy
  • Aligning digital activity to program and event goals
  • Identifying opportunities to grow reach and engagement
  • Advising on tools, platforms and best practice
☁️

CRM & Communications

  • Managing Social Planet CRM
  • Maintaining contact lists and segmentation
  • Streamlining community communications
  • Supporting data integrity and reporting
The Result

A thriving digital presence that serves the community

  • A fully maintained, up-to-date website that reflects the centre's programs and services
  • Consistent, engaging social media presence reaching more community members each month
  • Regular newsletters keeping hundreds of subscribers informed and connected
  • Clear analytics and reporting giving the team visibility of what's working
  • A cohesive digital strategy aligned to HLLC's community goals
  • Improved search visibility through ongoing SEO work

Case Study 5

Website redevelopment and marketing for an international audience

An upgraded e-commerce website, SEO, trade expo materials, a social media strategy and digital marketing training for Pilar Indonesia.

International DevelopmentE-commerce WebsiteSEO

Services: Website redevelopment · SEO · Marketing collateral · Social media strategy · Training

The Challenge

Reaching people beyond Indonesia

Pilar Indonesia relies on digital platforms, including its e-commerce website. It needed that website upgraded and easier for international audiences to find, consistent marketing materials for trade expos, and a clear plan for its social media channels.

What I Did

Website, search, print and social

  • Managed the redevelopment of the e-commerce website, upgrading its design and functionality
  • Implemented SEO strategies to reach an international audience
  • Created brand-consistent banners, flyers and catalogues for Indonesian trade expos
  • Developed and implemented a social media marketing strategy across multiple platforms
  • Led four online sessions on the basics of digital marketing for a youth group
The Result

One consistent presence, online and in person

  • An e-commerce website with upgraded design and functionality, optimised for international search
  • A matching set of branded materials ready for trade expos
  • A social media strategy in place across multiple platforms
  • Well-received training that built digital skills among young people

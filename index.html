About Blue Slate Consulting

Digital marketing built for the community sector

Blue Slate Consulting is a Sydney-based digital marketing consultancy working with NGOs, social enterprises and community organisations across Australia and Asia. We understand the realities of the sector: small teams, limited budgets and the need to demonstrate impact.

Our approach combines clear strategy with practical, hands-on delivery, so your team gets results it can maintain.

Lalitha Edupally, Founder

Lalitha brings a background in community development and extensive experience across the not-for-profit sector. Before founding Blue Slate Consulting, she worked as an independent consultant with organisations in Australia and Asia, including those in the case studies below.
